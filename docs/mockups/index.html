Dot Tracker mockups

These are the screens of the first version, drawn the way they'd look on a phone and on a computer. The book names come from the spreadsheet. The checkmarks, progress and ratings are made up to show how things look once someone has used the app for a while. Tap a page number, a rating or an Own box to try it.

On a phone

Four tabs along the bottom take you to your shelf, the full list of books, the people in your group and your wishlist, and your initials at the top open your account. Opening a book shows everything about it on one screen.

On a computer

The same app on a wider screen puts the filters, the list and the open book side by side, so you can go down the list checking things off without leaving it.
